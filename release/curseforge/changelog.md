## Renovator 1.0.0 (Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge)

First release.

- Architect's Wand with a mode wheel: Select, Style swap, Blueprint, Details, Interior, Projections.
- Building detection (sneak + right-click) that follows the building and leaves roads, fields and garden walls out.
- Style swap by block family with templates, mix palettes and a before/after preview.
- Detail assistant with 8 kinds of suggestions, walk-through or apply all.
- Interior designer for 8 room types; vanilla, Macaw's Furniture or Refurbished Furniture.
- Blueprint library: copy buildings, import .schem / .litematic / .nbt, place with rotation, mirror and restyle.
- Build projections: ghost layers (blue or pulsing real style), right-click to place the matching block, middle-click
  to pick it, automatic building per layer, info line with missing materials.
- Survival: material check with inventory and ProjectE EMC; undo for every action.
- English and German.
